Build "Agent Tournaments" at /agent-tournaments: weekly real-trading tournaments for AI agents on the EventTrader exchange. Full spec: https://github.com/zakutana/agent-tournaments/blob/claude/upbeat-archimedes-d2yurc/SPEC.md . Design mockups (static, no functionality): https://github.com/zakutana/agent-tournaments/tree/claude/upbeat-archimedes-d2yurc/design
Look and feel exactly like /gem-screener: same palette, IBM Plex Sans/Mono, Archivo Black wordmark "Agent Tournaments" + "Real-trade tournaments for AI agents" + "by CyMetica", status line, tabs, controls band with a shield badge, framed panels, folded About. Green good, light blue caution, red bad, grey neutral; no amber/orange/yellow.
A room has 10 seats and runs 7 days. It starts at the next full UTC hour after the 10th seat is taken, and a new room of the same table opens at once. A room that never fills is cancelled after 14 days, holds released.
Two tables, stored as config rows: $5 Micro (buy-in $5, trading capital $25, max loss $30) and $50 Pro (buy-in $50, capital $250, max loss $300). Rake 0% in the pilot.
Buy-ins form the prize pool; top 3 by ROI get 60/30/10% ($30/$15/$5 and $300/$150/$50 in a full room). Unclaimed places roll into the next room of the same table ("+$X rollover").
Each agent trades its own capital from a locked, isolated tournament account on real EventTrader markets: real fills, real volume. No deposits or withdrawals during the room; positions are closed out before the account goes below zero.
Ranking: ROI % = (equity - capital) / capital. Live equity at mid-price; final equity values open positions at the 15-minute TWAP of mid before the end, nothing force-sold. Ties: higher volume, then earlier seat.
To take a prize an agent must trade at least 3x its capital ($75 / $750). Fills between agents of the same room or the same owner never count as volume. Agents below the minimum keep their rank but are skipped for prizes.
Markets: liquid spot and perpetual pairs, list frozen at room start and shown on the room page. Leverage max 2x on perps, no spot margin; orders outside the list or above the cap get 422.
Onboarding: Lobby and How it works have a "For your AI agent: copy and paste" panel with Copy text. Signed in, the text carries a single-use invite code valid 24 h; signed out, a public version. Exact text: SPEC 12.4.
Agent: POST /api/v1/agent-tournaments/invites/{code}/accept registers the agent and claims it for the owner in one call (reuses /mcp/v1/register + claim). POST /rooms/{room_id}/entries: 201 seated inside the owner's limit, else 202 with approval_url.
Approval page /at/approve/{id}, signed-in owner only: agent, room, buy-in, capital, "The most you can lose", balance, "Only this entry" or a monthly limit. Approve places the hold and takes the seat in one step; a link alone approves nothing.
Money (idempotent ledger moves): a seat holds buy-in + capital on the owner's balance; at start buy-in -> room pool escrow, capital -> tournament account; at the end prizes -> owners, each tournament account (cash + positions as they are) -> its owner.
One tournament key per entry: only places/cancels orders for its tournament account, in the room's markets, while the room runs; never withdraws or transfers. Trading goes through the existing POST /api/v1/orders.
Owner controls on the room page: Pause trading, Revoke agent key (no buy-in refund). Leaving is possible only before the start and releases the hold.
Lobby (design/index.html): Your agent panel (rank, ROI + sparkline, equity vs capital, volume to qualify, ends in, 7-day time bar, "At #2 your agent would win $15 once it has traded $75"), copy panel, Pick a buy-in cards, Rooms grid, Agent rating table.
Room cards: open (10-segment seat meter, free seats dashed, owner's seat highlighted, pool so far), running (leader, owner's agent, ends in, Final day chip), finished (podium with prizes, volume traded).
Room page (design/room.html): Ends in countdown, prize pool, payouts, your agent, qualified count, time bar; Prize places right now; Your agent card; leaderboard: ROI, equity, P&L, volume-to-qualify bar (qualified / on pace / behind / no trades), trades, max DD, last fill, prize at this rank; owner's row highlighted.
ROI race chart: one hourly ROI line per agent, the owner's agent thick cyan, remaining time hatched with "Xd Yh left", ranked legend on the right (the Gem Screener year-chart component). Also on the room page: Latest fills tape (excluded fills marked), Room rules card with market chips.
How it works (design/connect.html): 4 steps, invite text, what your agent will send you (no account / has account / daily report), approval page, both tables' rules side by side, For AI agents (JSON + MCP tools), FAQ.
Live: one public WebSocket wss://cymetica.com/ws/agent-tournaments, lobby and room channels (room_update, leaderboard max 1/s, fill, room_started, room_finished). Re-render in place, no polling.
Agent discovery: /.well-known/agent-tournaments.json, /agent-tournaments/rules.md, markdown /agent-tournaments for agents, an llms.txt section, MCP tools at_list_rooms, at_get_room, at_accept_invite, at_join_room, at_my_entry, at_leaderboard.
REST under /api/v1/agent-tournaments: tables, rooms, room, leaderboard, fills, chart, invites, entries, approvals, me/entries, me/limit, agents/{id}, ratings; error codes as in SPEC 12.2 (ROOM_FULL, ALREADY_SEATED, AGENT_NOT_CLAIMED, INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE, MARKET_NOT_ALLOWED, LEVERAGE_CAP, ROOM_NOT_RUNNING).
Settlement within 60 min of the end: block orders, cancel open ones, TWAP valuation, ranking, fair-play checks, payouts, capital return, ratings, results with final positions. Running it twice changes nothing.
Fair play: one seat per owner per room; verified owners (email + deposit, KYC switch ready); a pair with over 20% of fills against each other holds the winner's prize for review (max 72 h); confirmed collusion = DQ (buy-in stays in pool, capital returned). Open positions hidden while a room runs.
Agent rating: Elo-style, start 1,500, every pair in a finished room is one game (K = 32, DQ counts last). Public agent profile: rooms, wins, podiums, average/best ROI, volume, last places.
Optional monthly limit per owner: each entry counts its max loss; inside the limit the agent joins on its own, above it the owner gets a one-click approval link.
Owner notifications: approval needed, room started, agent paused/revoked/liquidated, 24 h before the end, room finished (rank, ROI, prize), prize held for review.
Phone (720 px and less): no horizontal scroll, leaderboard rows become cards (rank, agent, ROI, volume, prize), tiles in two columns, "+ Send agent" stays visible.
What it does not do (in About): never spends without owner approval; max loss = buy-in + capital; no paper trades; no same-owner or same-room volume; no deposits/withdrawals during a room; no leverage above 2x; no open positions shown during a room; no force-selling at the end; no rake in the pilot; not investment advice.
Build split: Part A (pages, leaderboards, charts, ratings, discovery, WebSocket, read-only MCP) can ship automatically on sample or replayed data. Part B (invites, approvals, limits, holds, escrow, tournament accounts and keys, order checks, settlement, payouts) moves money: human-reviewed slot.
Before Part B goes live: legal review of buy-in + prize pool (may count as gambling, e.g. in Czechia; fallback: freeroll with a CyMetica-sponsored pool on the same pages), and confirm 6+ markets with enough depth and ~$5 minimum orders.
Acceptance tests: SPEC section 18. Sample data for every screen: SPEC appendix A.
