Build "Agent Tournaments" at /agent-tournaments to match the design in the brief: weekly trading tournaments for AI agents on Cymetica. Design brief: https://github.com/zakutana/agent-tournaments/blob/1f9566a23319b5d942cb8a64e0e22825824c60f4/SPEC.md . Clickable mockup of all 7 screens: https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/zakutana/agent-tournaments/blob/1f9566a23319b5d942cb8a64e0e22825824c60f4/design/preview.html . We give you the design and copy; the prompt words and how you build it are yours.
The idea: every table is a poker table with 10 seats. 10 AI agents, 7 days, 1 pot, best ROI % takes it. Stakes $5 and $50. Pick a table, take a seat one of 3 ways, approve once, watch.
Rules ($5 / $50 table): buy-in to the pot $5 / $50; trading capital $25 / $250 (yours, back after 7 days +- P&L); max loss $30 / $300; top 3 get 60/30/10%; to take a prize trade 3x the capital ($75 / $750), else the place passes on. Starts when the 10th seat is taken. No rake in the pilot.
Cymetica only: agents trade only on Cymetica's own order books; trades anywhere else never count. Live mode is real trading, so every table makes real volume.
The owner approves every entry (one click or a monthly auto limit); the agent never spends on its own. One seat per owner per table; one copy of each Cymetica bot per table; leverage 2x max; same-owner or same-table trades don't count as volume.
While a table runs only the owner sees their agent's trades and open positions; everyone sees rank, ROI and volume.
Paper mode: follow the account's PAPER / LIVE switch. Paper mode shows only paper tournaments and runs everything on the paper account (buy-in, capital, prizes, balances, trades, clones, bots); live mode only live ones; never mixed. Same rules and screens; prompts say "paper table ... with my paper account".
Flow: Lobby -> pick a table -> Take a seat -> (a) copy a prompt, your AI takes the seat and asks you once to OK the buy-in (your own approval flow, no extra screen), or (b) Manual setup: pick a bot and confirm on one screen -> Live table -> Results.
Look: cymetica.com colours only (navy #0a0e17 / #0d1520, cyan #00cfff, green #00ff88, red #ff4444, blue #3b82f6; no amber, orange, yellow). Fonts Unbounded, Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono. Big numbers, few words. Tokens: design/assets/at.css.
Header: logo (poker chip with bot eyes), Lobby, Top agents, How it works, LIVE + money in pots, your pill (one agent: avatar, rank, ROI; several: stacked avatars, count, total P&L). No action button: you always start by picking a table.
Lobby: hero "10 AI agents. 7 days. 1 pot. Best ROI takes it.", $5 -> $30 and $50 -> $300, PICK A TABLE, "1 pick a table > 2 copy a prompt > 3 approve", "No AI agent? Clone one of ours or pick a bot yourself".
With one seat: Your agent card (rank #2/10 and ROI big, sparkline, ends in, wins now, volume bar, "$27 more volume to lock it", WATCH). Then Tables (filters All / $5 / $50 / Mine) and Top agents (top 5 by Elo).
With 2+ seats the hero card becomes Your agents: stacked avatars, "10 agents, 10 tables", P&L now and Wins now big, one ROI bar per live agent, In play, Best, Needs you, ALL 10.
Then a Your agents board, a row per seat (filter All / Live / Waiting): needs-your-OK first (blue, APPROVE $300 right in the row), live by ROI (agent, table + day n/7, rank, ROI, volume bar, wins now, ends in), waiting last. A row opens its table.
Table cards are mini poker tables. Live: 10 avatars, crown on the leader, your seat glowing YOU, POT + ENDS IN, FINAL DAY on day 7. Open: dashed "+" free seats, N SEATS LEFT, your seat waiting or "your OK? APPROVE". Done: dimmed, crowned winner, PAID $X GG.
Take a seat: OPEN 3 SEATS LEFT; big table with free seats glowing "+ OPEN"; The deal: buy-in, capital, max you can lose (highlighted), 1st prize, min volume, CYMETICA ONLY. Then "3 ways in, pick one".
Way 1, Your agent: "Claude, ChatGPT, OpenClaw or your own bot joins this table", the prompt, COPY PROMPT (copies all, turns into "COPIED, PASTE IT TO YOUR AI"), invite code + 24h.
Way 2, Clone our agent: no bot picker on the page; the prompt tells your AI to pick a Cymetica bot by its backtest (one not at the table yet), clone it and seat it. COPY PROMPT.
Way 3, Manual setup, no AI: the 3 best bots by backtest return and PICK FROM 11 BOTS.
Prompts (brief 6): you write the words; mockup text is a placeholder. One block to copy per open table: table link, buy-in, capital, max loss, min volume, owner's invite code; with it any AI agent takes the seat itself. Owner approves the buy-in once. The agent never spends on its own, never asks for a password or key, trades only on Cymetica with the table's capital, reports rank and ROI daily. Clone our agent: the agent picks the bot by backtest. Paper mode: prompt says paper table and paper account.
Manual setup, one screen. Left: filter All / Crypto / Stocks, sort Return / Win rate / Drawdown; per bot: name, BLUE UPSIDE or RED DOWNSIDE, category, equity curve, backtest return + period, win rate, Sharpe, max drawdown, trades, clones; one already at the table dimmed.
Manual setup, right: "<bot> takes seat 4", mini table with the bot in the seat, buy-in, capital, max you can lose, balance, APPROVE $30 / BACK. Data: your backtest leaderboard behind /perpetual/agents (GET /api/v1/backtest/leaderboard).
Live table: LIVE day 4/7; big table with avatar, rank badge and ROI per seat, your seat pulsing YOU; POT, ticking countdown, payouts in the middle; Your agent (rank, ROI, wins now, volume, "Where +8.44% came from"); If it ended now.
Leaderboard: rank, avatar, name with model or clone type and Elo, big ROI, volume ("✓ $142" or "$48/75"), prize at this rank, your row cyan, filter All / Qualified.
Your trades (owner only): tiles Start $25.00 + Closed trades +$0.24 + Open now +$1.91 - Fees $0.04 = Now $27.11 +8.44%; fills newest first (time, BUY/SELL or LONG/CLOSE, market, size @ price, value, fee, P&L or OPEN + P&L), All N trades; Open now (entry -> now, P&L).
The race chart (you cyan with a ring at each of your trades, leader light grey, field dark grey, time left hatched) and rule chips led by "Trades on Cymetica only".
Results: winner in the middle; Your result (place, ROI, capital back, PLAY AGAIN); Paid out 1-3 with the line when a place passed down; Final standings; Your trades, final, positions handed back at the final price.
How it works: "Pick a table. Send your agent."; 4 steps; PICK A TABLE; stakes; four one-line answers.

Agents finding Cymetica on their own (brief 7): llms.txt, agent card (/.well-known/agent.json, skill near the top), MCP (initialize instructions, tool listing open tables), /build and /api-docs say on the first screen, in numbers: win $5 -> $30, $50 -> $300, owner's max loss, free seats now, top agents, first paper table free. Gem Screener stays put. The agent asks for a seat itself, no page needed; owner approves with one link. Done when a new agent gets from Cymetica's entry to a seat request in a few calls. Words, tools, flow: yours.
Left to you: registration and the invite code, approvals and limits, seating a picked bot, where the money sits, settlement and payouts, how ROI, volume and the final value (last 15-min average) are measured, anti-collusion, trade history, live updates, agent API/MCP, paper bookkeeping, prompt words, how an agent gets to a table and its owner on its own, what happens to a table that doesn't fill.
Check early: legal review of buy-in + prize pool (may count as gambling, e.g. in Czechia; a free sponsored table keeps the design); clones and bots trade 5-minute rounds today, so 7-day seats need a tournament mode (hide those cards until then) and 10 agents may need a higher bot limit; enough market depth for 3x volume.
