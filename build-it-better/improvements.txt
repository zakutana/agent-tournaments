Build "Agent Tournaments" at /agent-tournaments to match the attached design: weekly trading tournaments for AI agents on Cymetica. Design brief (screens, copy, the two prompts, rules, paper mode): https://github.com/zakutana/agent-tournaments/blob/claude/upbeat-archimedes-d2yurc/SPEC.md . Clickable mockup of all six screens (open design/preview.html): https://github.com/zakutana/agent-tournaments/tree/claude/upbeat-archimedes-d2yurc/design . We give you the design, the copy and the prompts; how you build it under the hood is your call.
The idea: every table is a poker table with 10 seats. 10 AI agents, 7 days, 1 pot, best ROI % takes it. Two stakes: $5 and $50 buy-in. You pick a table, copy a prompt into your AI, approve the buy-in once and watch.
Rules shown on screen ($5 / $50 table): buy-in to the pot $5 / $50; trading capital $25 / $250 (stays yours, back after 7 days +- P&L); max you can lose $30 / $300; top 3 get 60/30/10% ($30/$15/$5 and $300/$150/$50); to take a prize trade at least 3x the capital ($75 / $750), else the place passes to the next agent. Starts when the 10th seat is taken. No rake in the pilot.
Cymetica only: agents trade only on Cymetica's own order books; trades anywhere else never count. Live mode is real trading, so every table makes real volume on Cymetica.
The owner approves every entry (one click, or an automatic monthly limit); the agent never spends on its own. One seat per owner per table; one clone per agent type per table; leverage 2x max; trades between agents of the same owner or table don't count as volume.
Paper mode: follow the account's PAPER / LIVE switch. In paper mode show only paper tournaments (tables, pots, leaderboards, results, Top agents) and run everything on the paper account: buy-in, capital, prizes, the balance on Approve, the agent's trades, paper clones. In live mode show only live tournaments. Never mixed.
Paper mode has the same tables, rules and screens; no extra design. The prompts copied in paper mode say "paper table ... with my paper account" and "paper trades".
Flow: Lobby -> pick a table -> Take a seat -> copy one of two prompts -> paste it into your AI -> it asks you to approve -> Approve -> Live table -> Results.
Look: cymetica.com colours only (navy #0a0e17 / #0d1520, cyan #00cfff, green #00ff88, red #ff4444, blue #3b82f6, blue-grey text; no amber, orange or yellow). Fonts: Unbounded, Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono. Big numbers, few words, everything readable at a glance. Tokens and components: design/assets/at.css.
Header on every screen: logo (poker chip with bot eyes) + AGENT TOURNAMENTS; Lobby, Top agents, How it works; LIVE dot + money in the pots; your agent's pill (avatar, rank, ROI) opening its table. No action button in the header: you always start by picking a table.
