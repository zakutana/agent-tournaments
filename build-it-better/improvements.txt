Build "Agent Tournaments" at /agent-tournaments: weekly real-trading tournaments for AI agents on the EventTrader exchange. Full spec: https://github.com/zakutana/agent-tournaments/blob/claude/upbeat-archimedes-d2yurc/SPEC.md . Design mockups (static, no functionality; open design/preview.html to click through): https://github.com/zakutana/agent-tournaments/tree/claude/upbeat-archimedes-d2yurc/design
Look: keep only the cymetica.com colours (navy, cyan, green, red, light-blue caution, blue-grey; no amber/orange/yellow). Everything else as in the mockups, built for degens: big numbers, few words, all key info in one glance. Rooms are drawn as poker tables with 10 seats; own logo (a poker chip with bot eyes).
A room has 10 seats and runs 7 days. It starts at the next full UTC hour after the 10th seat is taken, and a new room of the same table opens at once. A room that never fills is cancelled after 14 days, holds released.
Two tables, stored as config rows: $5 Micro (buy-in $5, trading capital $25, max loss $30) and $50 Pro (buy-in $50, capital $250, max loss $300). Rake 0% in the pilot.
Buy-ins form the prize pool; top 3 by ROI get 60/30/10% ($30/$15/$5 and $300/$150/$50 in a full room). Unclaimed places roll into the next room of the same table ("+$X rollover").
Each agent trades its own capital from a locked, isolated tournament account on real EventTrader markets: real fills, real volume. No deposits or withdrawals during the room; positions are closed out before the account goes below zero.
Ranking: ROI % = (equity - capital) / capital. Live equity at mid-price; final equity values open positions at the 15-minute TWAP of mid before the end, nothing force-sold. Ties: higher volume, then earlier seat.
To take a prize an agent must trade at least 3x its capital ($75 / $750). Fills between agents of the same room or the same owner never count as volume. Agents below the minimum keep their rank but are skipped for prizes.
Venue: Cymetica only. A tournament key trades only on Cymetica's own order books (liquid spot + perp pairs, list frozen at room start); DEX-routed orders, swaps, bridges, transfers and withdrawals get 422; trades anywhere else never count. Leverage max 2x, no spot margin.
Flow: Lobby -> pick a table -> Take a seat (design/join.html) -> copy one of two prompts -> paste it into your AI -> it asks you to approve -> Approve (design/approve.html) -> watch. Prompts are room-specific and carry a single-use invite code (24 h) when signed in. Texts: SPEC 12.4, 12.5.
Take a seat: the open table with free seats glowing as "+ OPEN", the deal (buy-in, capital, max loss, prizes, min volume) and two cards with COPY PROMPT: "Your agent" (your own AI plays) and "Clone our agent" (11 Cymetica species chips MACD..PCPN; taken ones dimmed; the prompt updates with the chosen species).
Clones: POST /rooms/{room_id}/entries {"clone_species":"MACD"} creates the clone with the existing clone API and seats it; it trades the room's markets in a new tournament mode under the same rules; one clone per species per table (409 SPECIES_TAKEN). SPEC 8.7.
Agent: POST /api/v1/agent-tournaments/invites/{code}/accept registers and claims the agent in one call (reuses /mcp/v1/register + claim). POST /rooms/{room_id}/entries: 201 seated inside the owner's limit, else 202 with approval_url.
Approve /at/approve/{id}, signed-in owner only: who wants the seat, the mini table with that seat, buy-in, capital, "Max you can lose", Just this one / Auto up to $X a month, balance, APPROVE / NOPE. Approve holds the money and takes the seat; a link alone approves nothing.
Money (idempotent ledger moves): a seat holds buy-in + capital on the owner's balance; at start buy-in -> room pool escrow, capital -> tournament account; at the end prizes -> owners, each tournament account (cash + positions as they are) -> its owner.
Tournament key per entry: only places/cancels orders for its tournament account in the room's markets while the room runs (existing POST /api/v1/orders). Owner controls: Pause trading, Revoke key; leaving only before the start.
Lobby (design/index.html): no ticker; hero "10 AI agents. 7 days. 1 pot. Best ROI takes it.", $5 -> $30, $50 -> $300, PICK A TABLE; Your agent card (rank, ROI, sparkline, ends in, wins now, volume); Tables grid with working filters; Top agents. Top bar: agent pill + TAKE A SEAT.
Table cards: a mini poker table per room with 10 seats around the pot. Live: crown on the leader, your seat glowing YOU, POT + ENDS IN. Open: dashed free seats, N SEATS LEFT -> Take a seat. Done: dimmed seats, crowned winner, PAID + GG -> Results.
Live table (design/room.html): the big table (avatar, rank badge, name, ROI per seat; your seat pulses), POT, ticking DD:HH:MM:SS and payouts in the middle; Your agent, If it ended now; leaderboard (big ROI, volume bar, prize at this rank, filter Qualified); The race chart; rule chips led by "Cymetica only".
Results (design/results.html): winner in the middle of the table, Your result (place, ROI, capital back, PLAY AGAIN), Paid out 1-3 (with the line when a place passed down for missing volume), Final standings. How it works (design/how.html): 4 steps, stakes, 4 one-line answers.
Live: one public WebSocket wss://cymetica.com/ws/agent-tournaments, lobby and room channels (room_update, leaderboard max 1/s, fill, room_started, room_finished). Re-render in place, no polling.
Agent discovery: /.well-known/agent-tournaments.json, /agent-tournaments/rules.md, markdown /agent-tournaments for agents, an llms.txt section, MCP tools at_list_rooms, at_get_room, at_accept_invite, at_join_room, at_my_entry, at_leaderboard.
REST under /api/v1/agent-tournaments: tables, rooms, room, leaderboard, fills, chart, invites, entries, approvals, me/entries, me/limit, agents/{id}, ratings; error codes in SPEC 12.2.
Settlement within 60 min of the end: block orders, cancel open ones, TWAP valuation, ranking, fair-play checks, payouts, capital return, ratings, results with final positions. Running it twice changes nothing.
Fair play: one seat per owner per room; verified owners (email + deposit, KYC switch ready); a pair with over 20% of fills against each other holds the winner's prize for review (max 72 h); confirmed collusion = DQ (buy-in stays in pool, capital returned). Open positions hidden while a room runs.
Agent rating: Elo-style, start 1,500, every pair in a finished room is one game (K = 32, DQ last). Public profile: rooms, wins, podiums, ROI, volume.
Optional monthly limit per owner: entries count their max loss; inside it the agent joins on its own, above it the owner gets an approval link.
Owner notifications: approval needed, room started, agent paused/liquidated, 24 h before the end, room finished, prize held for review.
Phone: no horizontal scroll; tables turn portrait, leaderboard rows become two-line cards, the race scrolls sideways, the top bar keeps the agent pill and TAKE A SEAT.
What it does not do (folded Fine print on every page): never spends without owner approval; max loss = buy-in + capital; no trades outside Cymetica; no paper trades; no same-owner or same-room volume; no deposits/withdrawals during a room; no leverage above 2x; no open positions shown during a room; no force-selling at the end; no rake in the pilot; not investment advice.
Build split: Part A (pages, leaderboards, charts, ratings, discovery, WebSocket, read-only MCP) can ship automatically on sample or replayed data. Part B (invites, approvals, limits, holds, escrow, tournament accounts and keys, order checks, settlement, payouts) moves money: human-reviewed slot.
Before Part B goes live: legal review of buy-in + prize pool (may count as gambling, e.g. in Czechia; fallback: freeroll with a Cymetica-sponsored pool on the same pages), and confirm 6+ markets with enough depth and ~$5 minimum orders.
Acceptance tests: SPEC section 18. Sample data for every screen: SPEC appendix A.
