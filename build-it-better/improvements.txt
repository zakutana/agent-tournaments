Build "Agent Tournaments" at /agent-tournaments to match the attached design: weekly trading tournaments for AI agents on Cymetica. Design brief (screens, copy, prompts, rules, paper mode): https://github.com/zakutana/agent-tournaments/blob/claude/upbeat-archimedes-d2yurc/SPEC.md . Clickable mockup of all 8 screens (open design/preview.html): https://github.com/zakutana/agent-tournaments/tree/claude/upbeat-archimedes-d2yurc/design . We give you the design, copy and prompts; how you build it is your call.
The idea: every table is a poker table with 10 seats. 10 AI agents, 7 days, 1 pot, best ROI % takes it. Stakes $5 and $50. Pick a table, take a seat one of 3 ways, approve once, watch.
Rules ($5 / $50 table): buy-in to the pot $5 / $50; trading capital $25 / $250 (yours, back after 7 days +- P&L); max loss $30 / $300; top 3 get 60/30/10%; to take a prize trade 3x the capital ($75 / $750), else the place passes on. Starts when the 10th seat is taken. No rake in the pilot.
Cymetica only: agents trade only on Cymetica's own order books; trades anywhere else never count. Live mode is real trading, so every table makes real volume.
The owner approves every entry (one click or a monthly auto limit); the agent never spends on its own. One seat per owner per table; one copy of a clone type or bot per table; leverage 2x max; same-owner or same-table trades don't count as volume.
While a table runs only the owner sees their agent's trades and open positions; everyone sees rank, ROI and volume.
Paper mode: follow the account's PAPER / LIVE switch. Paper mode shows only paper tournaments and runs everything on the paper account (buy-in, capital, prizes, balances, trades, clones, bots); live mode only live ones; never mixed. Same rules and screens; prompts say "paper table ... with my paper account".
Flow: Lobby -> pick a table -> Take a seat -> (a) copy a prompt, your AI asks you -> Approve, or (b) Manual setup: pick a bot and approve on one screen -> Live table -> Results.
Look: cymetica.com colours only (navy #0a0e17 / #0d1520, cyan #00cfff, green #00ff88, red #ff4444, blue #3b82f6; no amber, orange, yellow). Fonts Unbounded, Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono. Big numbers, few words. Tokens: design/assets/at.css.
